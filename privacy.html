Clips on Clips: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

"Clips on Clips" is a private tool used only by its owner to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It has no other users.

What data it uses

  • When the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic account information (the account's display name). These are used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • The token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent anywhere except back to TikTok.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about anyone else.
  • It does not sell, share or publish any data.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking, and it has no analytics.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Apps), and can delete the stored token from their computer.
