Clips on Clips: Terms of Service

Last updated: 17 November 2026

"Clips on Clips" is a private, personal tool. It is used only by its owner to send short videos that the owner has made to the owner's own TikTok account, where the owner reviews and publishes them in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the Clips on Clips accounts uses it.

What it does

When the owner chooses a video, the tool uploads it to the owner's TikTok inbox through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Nothing is published without the owner finishing the post in the TikTok app.

Content

The owner is responsible for the videos sent with the tool and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Each video credits the streamer it comes from.
